# Film Edge — round 2

Connor selected Film Edge from the first set and requested a substantially thicker left blade, a slimmer right blade, and a true overlapping junction. The left blade is in front of the right, with distinct teal tones to show depth.

## Concepts

- A / Broad overlap: closest to the updated hand sketch; straight, broad left blade and slim rear blade.
- B / Tapered blades: narrower rear blade, more open stance, and shorter film ribbon exposing more of the junction.
- C / Soft cut: eased upper corners and film notches, preserving a shared pointed join.

## Construction

The rear blade is a complete polygon rather than disconnected top and bottom pieces. The front blade is drawn last and occludes both the rear blade and the film at its left edge. Both blades meet at the same lower point; there is no separated foot or slot at the junction. The film has true transparent perforations. A vector mask prevents the hidden rear blade from filling those perforations.

Dark palette: film #04635F, front blade #71D7CD, rear blade #489F96. Light and single-color alternatives accompany each design, as does an isolated V for evaluating the blade construction. All individual marks use only vector geometry; no raster images, embedded fonts, or external resources.
